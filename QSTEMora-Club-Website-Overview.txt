This document explains everything about the QSTEMora Club website: who we are,
what we do, what each page contains, the subjects we cover, our team, the
schedule, and how to contact us or partner with us.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHO WE ARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QSTEMora Club (also called Qena Student Club, "QSC") is a student-run learning
platform built for STEM students across Egypt.

The club started with the Senior '26 students of Qena STEM School. They came
from different governorates and faced many problems and difficulties in
studying, so they decided to help other students by collecting study material
and explaining it in a clear, constructive way — both for schools of
outstanding students in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics
(STEM) and for general secondary schools.

Today the club is led by the Senior '28 team at STEM Qena, carrying the mission
forward from the deserts of Qena and connecting members and mentors across all
Egyptian governorates.

Our policy is simple: "The student always comes first. We always look for your
excellence and comfort."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT WE DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We provide structured, centralized access to high-quality study resources:

  • Learning Outcomes (LOs) — every subject is broken down into clear, numbered
    learning outcomes, each on its own lesson page.
  • Verified presentation slides (PowerPoint decks).
  • Video lectures.
  • Reference question banks.
  • Capstone resources — design guides, calendars, rubrics, and poster guides.
  • Competition guidance — ISEF, Science Olympiads, scholarships, and more.
  • In-person School Hall sessions for STEM Qena students.

All materials are curated by top Senior '28 students and revised by experienced
STEM teachers across Qena, which keeps everything structured, reliable, and
easy to use.

The Senior '28 leaders run every part of the club — design, content writing,
summarizing, public relations, and explaining.

Reach: 27 STEM governorates • 45+ Senior '26 & '28 mentors.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE WEBSITE — PAGE BY PAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The website is a static multi-page site (plain HTML, styled with Tailwind CSS
and a small custom stylesheet; a single shared script, app.js, handles the
menus and pop-up windows). The content lives directly in the pages, so it is
easy to read and edit.

  • Home (index.html)
      Landing page with the club intro, the School Hall session schedule, a
      preview of subjects, a preview of the team, and JOIN buttons.

  • Subjects (subjects.html)
      A catalog of all 16 subjects. You can filter by category, by semester,
      or search by name.

  • Subject (subject.html)
      One page per subject showing its list of Learning Outcomes as cards,
      plus a downloadable subject file (PDF) where available. Filterable by
      semester.

  • Lesson (lesson.html)
      A detail page for a single Learning Outcome, with three sections:
      Videos, Files, and Tests.

  • About (about.html)
      Our story, mission, and headline numbers.

  • Team members (team.html)
      Two branches (see section 6): Seniors '28 (current, with photos) and
      Seniors '26 (alumni).

  • Partners (partners.html)
      Our success partners — currently a "coming soon" placeholder.

Every page shares the same header (with a JOIN button) and footer (with contact
and social links).

4. SUBJECTS WE COVER (16)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Science / Core:
  • Geology      — earth systems, plate tectonics, mineralogy, stratigraphy,
                   and geological processes.
  • Math         — differential and integral calculus, linear algebra,
                   analytical geometry, and probability.
  • Chemistry    — thermodynamics, chemical kinetics, atomic theory,
                   equilibrium, and electrochemistry.
  • Biology      — cellular respiration, molecular genetics, biotechnology,
                   and physiological systems.
  • Mechanics    — Newtonian dynamics, 2D/3D statics, rotational equilibrium,
                   and kinematics.
  • Physics      — electromagnetism, wave optics, modern quantum physics, and
                   electric circuits.
  • Computer Science — algorithms, Python programming, data structures, and
                   computational thinking.

Languages:
  • English      — academic writing, scientific reading comprehension, critical
                   analysis, and vocabulary.
  • French (Française) — French grammar rules, DELF practice, and reading
                   comprehension.
  • German (Deutsch)   — German grammar cases, vocabulary building, and
                   Goethe A1–B1 preparation.
  • Arabic       — classical Arabic grammar (nahw & sarf), rhetoric (balagha),
                   and literature.

Humanities / Other:
  • Religion     — Islamic and Christian religious studies, spiritual values,
                   and moral ethics.
  • Social Studies — Egyptian modern history, geography of natural resources,
                   and civics.
  • Capstone     — Grand Challenges of Egypt design process, engineering design
                   journal, and prototyping.
  • Opportunities — ISEF, Science Olympiads, scholarships, quad charts, and
                   competition guidelines.
  • Arduino      — embedded C/C++, sensor interfacing, PWM control, circuit
                   wiring, and robotics.

Each subject is broken into numbered Learning Outcomes. Currently the Learning
Outcomes are populated for Semester 1 (Semester 2 is intentionally left empty
for now). Subjects that have a reference file also show a gold download banner.

9. TECHNICAL NOTES (FOR MAINTAINERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • The site is fully static HTML — no database and no server-side code.
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS (via CDN) + a custom stylesheet (css/styles.css).
  • Shared behavior (mobile menu, navigation highlight, JOIN and partnership
    pop-ups, member/partner detail pop-ups) lives in assets/app.js.
  • Site settings (WhatsApp link, contact email, contact phone) are stored in
    one place at the top of assets/app.js in "window.QSC_CONFIG" — change them
    there and the footer and JOIN buttons update automatically.
  • Content is edited directly in the HTML files; each card has a comment
    label above it explaining what it is.
  • Learning Outcome content is driven by query parameters
    (subject.html?s=..., lesson.html?id=...), so for reliable local testing the
    site should be served over HTTP (e.g. "python -m http.server") rather than
    opened directly as files.
  • Learning outcomes and lesson data were generated from a supplied hook-title
    table; capstone download files live in the capstone_files/ folder and team
    photos in the team_images/ folder.
