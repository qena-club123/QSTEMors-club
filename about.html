Innovative Way To Learn

Qena Student Club (QSC) • Founded in Upper Egypt

Welcome to the website of Qena Student Club (QSC) — built for STEM students across Egypt. The club is now led by the Senior '28 team at STEM Qena, carrying its mission forward from the deserts of Qena and connecting members and mentors across all Egyptian governorates. The materials are curated by top Senior '28 students and revised by experienced STEM teachers across Qena. QSTEMora provides structured, centralized access to learning outcomes (LOs), verified presentation slides, video lectures, and reference question banks. Our policy is simple: 'The student always comes first.'

Our Senior '28 leaders run every part of the club — from design and content writing to summarizing, public relations, and explaining. Together with experienced teachers across Qena, they craft the PowerPoint slides, record the video lectures, and build the reference question banks that keep QSTEMora structured, reliable, and easy to use.

"Our policy is clear: The student always comes first. We always look for your excellence and comfort."
